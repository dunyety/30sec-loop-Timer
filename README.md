# 30s Loop Timer

A simple, no-fuss timer that counts down 30 seconds, beeps, and starts again on its own, with a built-in video player so you can follow a workout or face massage without leaving the page.

## Why I built this

I struggled to stay consistent with workouts and facial lymphatic drainage. As a beginner, I was constantly starting and stopping a timer, then jumping out of the video to deal with it, then going back to find my place again. Honestly, the switching back and forth was more tiring than the exercises themselves.

So I built this to put everything in one place: the timer and the video, side by side. Press Start once and just focus on the movement.

## Features

- 30-second countdown with a circular progress ring
- Loops automatically when it hits zero, with a round counter
- Three short beeps at the end of every round
- Play your own video (workout, face massage, anything) while the timer runs
- The video pauses when you pause the timer and rewinds when you reset
- Animated gradient background
- Plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript, no backend and nothing to install

## How to use

1. Keep `timer.html`, `style.css` and `script.js` together in one folder.
2. Open `timer.html` in your browser.
3. Tap **Choose video** and pick a video from your device.
4. Tap **Start**. The timer and video begin together.
5. Use **Pause** or **Reset** whenever you need to.
